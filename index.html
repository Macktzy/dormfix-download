How It Works

DormFix is a modern mobile application designed to streamline dormitory management by digitally connecting residents, admins, and maintenance staff in one centralized ecosystem.

Built for reliability, the app features a robust "smart sync" system that allows for real-time tracking of reports and payments when online, while ensuring that reports created offline are automatically uploaded the moment connectivity is restored.

By replacing outdated paper forms with this digital workflow, DormFix offers students total transparency on their request status, gives admins organized oversight of dorm operations, and provides staff with a clear, distraction-free job list, making dorm living safer and more efficient for everyone.
